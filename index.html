Yongqiang Chu (褚勇强)

Childress Klein Distinguished Professor · Professor of Finance Director, Childress Klein Center for Real Estate Belk College of Business, University of North Carolina at Charlotte

I study corporate finance, banking, and real estate. My recent work looks at how political beliefs and culture shape mortgage lending, housing markets, and corporate decisions, and at how AI and fintech change who gets credit.

My papers appear in the Journal of Finance, the Journal of Financial Economics, the Review of Financial Studies, Management Science, and the Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis. Before joining UNC Charlotte in 2018, I spent ten years on the finance faculty at the University of South Carolina.
